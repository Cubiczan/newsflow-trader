02 / 08

Trading the news by hand is too slow

Retail traders face four compounding problems that an autonomous agent can solve.
01 · LATENCY

Headlines move markets in seconds

An earnings beat, an FDA approval, a Fed dot-plot shift — by the time a human reads, digests, and clicks "buy", the move is over. Algorithmic desks trade in milliseconds; retail sits on the sidelines.

02 · SENTIMENT

"Is this bullish or bearish?" is hard

Most headlines are nuanced. "Tesla Q3 deliveries miss estimates, China competition intensifies" cuts both ways. A trader needs to read, weigh, and decide — and fatigue compounds over a trading day.

03 · DISCIPLINE

Risk limits slip when emotions spike

FOMO buys at the top, revenge trades after a loss, oversized positions on a "sure thing". Without a hard-coded risk guard, traders blow up accounts on a single bad day. Discipline doesn't scale manually.

04 · TRANSPARENCY

"What did the bot just do?"

Black-box algorithmic services execute trades without explaining why. Traders can't audit the reasoning, can't replay decisions, and can't learn from mistakes. The decision trail is invisible.

04 / 08

Inside one agent tick

The agent loop runs every 15–300 seconds (configurable). Each tick executes these 6 steps.
1

Wake up & fetch portfolio

Pull live account equity, cash, and open positions from Alpaca paper API. Refresh the watchlist (10 default tickers + macro).

2

Ingest fresh headlines

NewsFetcher pulls 1–3 new headlines per tick. Mock generator ships realistic AAPL / NVDA / TSLA / SPY / macro news; real sources drop in.

3

LLM sentiment analysis

Each headline → GLM-4.6 with a strict JSON-only system prompt. Returns label (bullish/bearish/neutral), score (-1..+1), confidence (0..1), reasoning.

4

Risk guard evaluation

Check max positions, max position %, sentiment threshold, paper-only. SELL only if we hold the symbol. BUY only if under caps. 8 orders blocked live.

5

Submit to Alpaca

Approved trades → market order via @alpacahq/alpaca-trade-api v4. Day TIF. Auto-cancels at close. client_order_id tagged for audit trail.

6

Persist + broadcast

Decision logged to SQLite (NewsItem + Decision + Position tables). socket.io emits every event live to the dashboard — judges see the full chain.

07 / 08

Risk guardrails — not just submitting what the LLM says

Every proposed trade passes a 4-check guard before reaching Alpaca. All thresholds are tunable live from the dashboard.
Max Positions
5
Hard cap on concurrent open positions. BUY orders that would exceed this are rejected. Tunable 1–20 from the dashboard.
Max Position Size
10%
No single position can exceed 10% of portfolio equity. Position sizing scales with sentiment confidence. Tunable 1–25%.
Sentiment Threshold
0.55
LLM confidence must be ≥ 0.55 to act. Below this, the agent logs HOLD and skips. Tunable 0.30–0.95.
Paper-Only
TRUE
Hard refusal of any live order, regardless of ALPACA_PAPER env value. Cannot be disabled from the UI.

Blocked during live run (8 orders)

  • SELL on TSLA, MSFT, AAPL — agent didn't hold these positions yet (can't sell what you don't have)
  • BUY on TSLA, GOOGL, META — max positions (5) reached, would exceed the cap
  • SELL on MARKET macro news — neutral sentiment, but the agent still proposed SELL on the macro bearish headline; risk guard correctly rejected because MARKET isn't a tradable ticker
  • HOLD on neutral macro news — logged as SKIPPED, no order sent
